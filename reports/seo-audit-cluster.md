**Cluster health score: 54/100.**

3. `vajinal-mikrobiyom-fiv` — Listed in navigation but `beslenme-yasam` has only 2 total spokes. The article's topic (microbiome and IVF success) is emerging but sits in an underdeveloped cluster.

**Top 3 weak hubs**

1. `tani-sureci` — Only 1 spoke (`yumurtlama-takibi`). The tanı funnel is the site's primary new-visitor entry point. It is missing AMH/FSH interpretation, spermiogram reading, and ultrasound evaluation articles.
2. `yas-ve-fertilite` — Zero article spokes. The hub exists but no articles feed into it, despite age-related fertility being a top-searched topic.
3. `hormon-paneli` — Zero article spokes. The hub is referenced throughout navigation as a key diagnostic resource but has nothing behind it.

**Top 5 content gaps**

1. AMH testi: referans aralıkları ve yorumlama — closes `tani-sureci` + `hormon-paneli` simultaneously.
2. Varikosel ve erkek kısırlığı — the single largest missing spoke in `erkek-infertilitesi`; varicocel is the most common surgically correctable male factor.
3. Sperm DNA fragmentasyonu — referenced in navigation descriptions but no article exists; needed in `erkek-infertilitesi` and `tani-sureci`.
4. Elective yumurta dondurma (sosyal dondurma) — `fertilite-koruma` covers cancer patients but not the larger elective-freezing audience.
5. IVF ilaçları rehberi (gonadotropinler, trigger shot) — `ilac-rehberi` hub has zero spokes; this gap means the hub page gets no PageRank flow from any article.
